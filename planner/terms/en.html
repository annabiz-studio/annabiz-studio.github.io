3. What the app does, and where your data is

The app helps you plan your day: routine, tasks, "monkeys" (the day's three priorities), ideas as bubbles, brain dump, reminders and notifications.

  • Your data is stored on your phone. It is not sent to us, except what you share in the family planner (section 4).
  • Android backup: if Android's automatic backup is on, the app's data and pictures are included in the backup to your Google account, according to your device settings.
  • Automatic backup to a folder: if you turn it on, the planner saves a copy of your data in the folder you choose, including a cloud storage folder. You are responsible for that folder, for who can access it, and for keeping it secure.
  • Widgets and notifications show task and monkey titles on your home screen and lock screen. Anyone who can see your phone can see them.

4. The family planner

  • What it is: you can create a family, or ask to join one with the family name and the code. The family admin approves every request to join. Members share tasks and "done" photos. Shared content is stored in the Google Firebase cloud service.
  • Membership is tied to the phone: it is recognised by the app's installation, with no username or password. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data disconnects the phone from the family, and we have no way to restore access. When the admin leaves, another member becomes admin (a member who can edit comes first). If no one is left, the family is deleted from the cloud.
  • Responsibility for content:
    • You are responsible for what you share (tasks, notes, names, photos). Share only content you are allowed to share.
    • Do not share offensive, harassing or unlawful content, or content that invades other people's privacy.
    • Share a photo of a person only with their consent, and a photo of a minor only with a parent's consent.
  • Removal and suspension: if we learn of content or use that breaks these terms, for example from a report to annabiz.studio@gmail.com, we may remove the content and suspend or block a family's or a member's access.
  • Deletion:
    • The family admin can delete the whole family from the cloud, including all members, tasks and photos.
    • Any member can delete their own data: their name, the tasks they created and the photos they sent.
    • Every deletion asks for confirmation twice. A deletion cannot be undone.
    • Content that has not been deleted stays in the cloud until it is deleted under this section or under the Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting inactive accounts:
    • A member who has not opened the family planner for 12 months in a row is removed from the family, with their data: their name, the tasks they created and the photos they sent.
    • A family that none of its members has opened for 12 months in a row is deleted entirely.
    • This deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
