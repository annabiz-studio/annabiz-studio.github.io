2. Made for children

The Game is designed for children. Every purchase, purchase restore, setting and link that leads outside the Game sits behind a "parental gate": a question a grown-up has to answer. The gate is there to stop accidental taps by children. It does not replace adult supervision.

3. In-game purchases

  • Some squishies are free. Extra packs and "Remove ads" are one-time purchases. There are no subscriptions and no recurring charges.
  • Purchases are made and charged only through Google Play, under Google Play's terms. The price that applies is the one shown in Google Play's payment window.

  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy and are requested from Google Play. If a refund is granted, the purchased content locks again.
  • The "Squishy of the day" and a squishy unlocked by watching a video are available for that one day only. They are not purchases and are not refundable.

The Game itself collects no personal data and has no analytics or tracking. The full privacy policy is published separately and on the Game's Google Play page, and it forms part of these terms.

5. Ads (from version 1.4.0)

  • Versions up to and including 1.3.1 contain no ads at all.
  • From version 1.4.0 the Game shows ads from Google AdMob, configured as child-directed: non-personalized ads only, with content rating G (suitable for all ages). We don't use the device's advertising ID for ad tracking.
  • Only two kinds of ads, and no banners:
    • Opt-in video: the child chooses to watch a video and, in return, a locked squishy opens for that day. Up to 3 videos a day.
    • Between-screens ad: at most one every few minutes of play. Never at launch, never in a new player's first minutes, and never inside an open window.
  • Players who bought "Remove ads" or any pack see no ads.
  • Ad content comes from Google, not from us. We are not responsible for ad content or for anything shown after tapping an ad. If you see an unsuitable ad, tell us and we will block it.
