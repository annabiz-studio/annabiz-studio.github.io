Purchases, settings and links that lead outside the Game are for adults only. If you are not the child's parent or guardian, do not accept these terms on the child's behalf.

2. Made for children

The Game is designed for children aged 8 and under, with parental supervision. Every purchase, purchase restore, setting and link that leads outside the Game sits behind a "parental gate": a question a grown-up has to answer. The gate is there to stop accidental taps by children. It is not age verification, and it does not replace adult supervision.

3. In-game purchases

  • 10 squishies are free forever. Extra packs, one pack with all 15, and "Remove ads" are one-time purchases. Buying any pack also removes ads. There are no subscriptions and no recurring charges.
  • Purchases are made and charged only through Google Play, under Google Play's terms. The price is shown before you buy, and the price that applies is the one in Google Play's payment window.
  • Every purchase requires passing the parental gate and confirming in Google Play's payment window. A purchase made this way is treated as made by the adult. You can require a password for every purchase in Google Play's settings.
  • A purchase belongs to the Google account that made it. You can restore it on another device with the same account through "Restore purchases" in the grown-ups panel.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy and are requested from Google Play, which processes the payment. If a refund is granted, the purchased content locks again. Nothing in these terms limits any cancellation or refund right the law gives consumers, including distance-contract withdrawal rights in Israel and the EU.
  • The "Squishy of the day" and a squishy unlocked by watching a video stay open until the end of that day (midnight). They are not purchases and are not refundable.

4. Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game has no sign-up, no chat, and no analytics or tracking. To show ads, the Google AdMob SDK collects limited technical data and shares it with Google, such as approximate location from the IP address, ad views and taps, diagnostics and device identifiers. This data is used for showing ads, fraud prevention and ad measurement only. Ads are not personalized and the Game is configured as child-directed. We do not build profiles of children. Full details are in the privacy policy, published separately and on the Game's Google Play page. Parents can contact us with any question, or to ask to see or delete data: annabiz.studio@gmail.com.

5. Ads

  • The Game shows ads only through Google AdMob, an ads SDK certified for Google Play's Families program. Ads are configured as child-directed: not personalized, with content rating G (suitable for all ages). We do not use the device's advertising ID for tracking, personalized ads or remarketing.
  • Only two kinds of ads, and no banners:
    • Opt-in video: watching is always the child's choice, and the button is clearly marked as a video. The offer can be closed without watching. In return for watching, a locked squishy opens until the end of the day. Up to 3 videos a day. Not watching takes nothing else away from the Game.
    • Between-screens ad: at most one every 4 minutes of play. Never at launch, never in a new player's first 10 minutes, and never while a window or the parental gate is open.
  • The Game works offline and without watching any ad.
  • Players who bought "Remove ads" or any pack see no ads at all.
  • Ad content comes from Google, not from us. If you see an unsuitable ad, tell us and we will block it.
