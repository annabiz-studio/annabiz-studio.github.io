Privacy Policy

App: ZooZoo Land (Android package com.yaron.zoozooland) · Last updated: September 29, 2026

🌐 All languages · כל השפות

ZooZoo Land is a game for children. We built it so that it does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no chat, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location, and it does not read any device identifier (such as the advertising ID).

What stays on the device

Your child's zoo (animals, the names the child gives them, decorations and settings) is saved only on the device, in the app's private storage. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases and the optional subscription are processed entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iPhone). We never receive payment details. Every purchase is behind a grown-ups-only gate. Google Play's and Apple's privacy policies apply to the payment itself.

Notifications

If a parent allows notifications, the game can show a local reminder that the daily surprise egg is ready. The reminder is created on the device; nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

A grown-up (after the grown-ups-only gate) can share a picture of the zoo using the phone's own share menu. The picture goes only where the grown-up chooses to send it; we do not receive it.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and similar laws. Because we collect no personal information, there is no personal information to review, delete or hand over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: annabiz.studio@gmail.com

כלום. אין חשבונות, אין צ'אט, אין פרסומות ואין כלי אנליטיקה או מעקב. המשחק לא מבקש שם, מייל, תמונות, אנשי קשר או מיקום, ולא קורא שום מזהה של המכשיר.
